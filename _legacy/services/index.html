Frequently asked questions

What's included in the price?

The hourly rate or package fee covers the shoot itself, editing, colour correction, and delivery via a private online gallery. You receive high-resolution files licensed for your intended use (web, print, marketing).

Do I need to pay a deposit?

For larger bookings (full-day events, multi-day product shoots) I usually ask for a 30% deposit to confirm the date. For shorter shoots an invoice after the work is fine.

How many photos will I get?

It varies by shoot type. A typical 4-hour event yields 100–200 edited images; a product shoot is priced by deliverable image count, agreed in advance.

Can I get the raw files?

I deliver finished, edited images. Raw files aren't included by default — that's an industry-standard practice that protects the quality you receive. If you have a specific reason to need them, talk to me.

What if it rains or the event is cancelled?

For weather-affected outdoor shoots we agree on a backup plan or reschedule. For cancellations, the deposit is generally retained but I'll always try to find a fair solution.

Do you photograph weddings?

Yes — small, simple weddings and ceremonies. My style is documentary, not heavily posed. Write to me with the date and venue and I'll send a tailored quote.
