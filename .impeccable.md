# Impeccable Design Context — raavi.ee

## Design Context

### Users
- **Prospective clients** (primary): Brands, event organizers, galleries, small businesses in Estonia/Tartu looking to hire a photographer. They arrive from Instagram, Google, or referral. High-intent visitors who need to quickly assess style fit and then book.
- **Editorial/agency clients** (secondary): Design studios, cultural institutions looking for a reliable documentary or product shooter. They assess professionalism and caliber quickly.
- **Returning clients**: Know the work, come back for pricing and to initiate a new project.

### Brand Personality
- **"Raavi" = storyteller in Farsi** — the name is the core brand meaning. Photography as quiet, precise storytelling.
- Three words: **precise, observant, unhurried**
- Emotional goal: confidence and trust. The client should feel they are hiring someone who will handle important moments without needing direction.
- Voice: terse, honest, no superlatives. "I work quietly, stay focused, and pay attention to the details that matter."

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Precise/utilitarian** — Swiss-influenced, optically rigorous. Design studio feel. Fits the photographer's stated background in design and model-making ("accuracy and clarity matter").
- **NOT**: warm/handmade, playful, maximalist, tech/startup, Framer-template minimal
- **Light mode** — the work is presented on a near-white surface so photography supplies all color. Strict greyscale palette is correct and should be preserved.
- **Portfolio scope**: Events and Products only — two categories, intentionally minimal.
- Anti-references: Squarespace/Framer portfolio templates, Poppins/Urbanist font combos, pill buttons with uppercase tracking

### Design Principles
1. **Photography first** — UI chrome must never compete with images. Every design decision should ask: does this make the photos more or less prominent?
2. **Precision over decoration** — No ornament for its own sake. Every visual element must earn its place.
3. **Narrative through restraint** — "Raavi = storyteller" is expressed through what's held back, not what's added. Sparse copy, unhurried layout, images that speak.
4. **Credibility through typography** — The typeface is the primary carrier of brand authority. It must feel considered, not templated.
5. **Conversion path is quiet but clear** — CTAs exist and are findable, but they don't shout. A client who is ready should be able to act without hunting.
